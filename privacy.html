Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Kenan Assistant is a private tool used by one person (its owner) for their own Google accounts. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses two read-only Google scopes: gmail.readonly (message headers, subject and Google's short snippet) and calendar.readonly (calendar events). It cannot send, delete or change email or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner summaries, meeting reminders and answers to the owner's own questions in a private Telegram chat. Summaries are generated with an AI model (Anthropic Claude) under the owner's own account; only the minimum text needed for a summary is sent.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the owner's private server. Email metadata from the last 7 days and calendar events for the next 14 days are kept, and older data is deleted automatically. Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest by the server's file permissions and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or for training AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
